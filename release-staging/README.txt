Pricey 0.1.0 - "Know exactly what your product costs. Know exactly what to charge."
Clever Signal - universal product pricing, costing & profitability for makers, resellers and small manufacturers.

TO RUN
  1. Extract this zip to any folder (keep the folder structure).
  2. Double-click START_PRICEY.bat.

Your data is stored on your own computer in:
  %APPDATA%\Pricey\
Nothing is sent to the internet. No account needed.

BACKUPS
  Use Settings > Backup inside the app to save a backup file,
  and Settings > Restore to bring one back.

SUPPORT FILES
  Pricey.exe + resources/  - the application itself (do not move files)
  START_PRICEY.bat - launcher (this file's folder is the install location)
